02 / SWITCH WITH INTENTION

Only when
you mean it.

Hold a key. Touch the edge. You’re there.

Option + the screen edge is the default. Choose your own modifier, or use the familiar push-and-wait gesture. Corners stay clear for macOS window controls.

⌥ option+screen edge →

A cursor cue confirms the switch. A menu-bar mark keeps connection status a glance away.

03 / A GOOD MATCH FOR YOUR DESK?

Small setup.
Clear expectations.

01

Give Mux a home on both Macs.

Install the same build in Applications on your desktop Mac and MacBook. Follow the permission guide.

02

Introduce the two sides.

Use one private pairing code. Choose the Mac connected to your keyboard, then identify your mouse’s channel on each Mac.

03

Make the edge your own.

Choose the outside edge on each desk. Hold your modifier and move across. Turn on launch at login to settle in.
